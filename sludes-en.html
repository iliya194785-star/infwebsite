Painter

Vincent van Gogh

Tormented genius · Master of Post-Impressionism

Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch painter and one of the most influential figures in the history of Western art. In just over a decade he created more than 2,000 works, including The Starry Night, Sunflowers, and numerous self-portraits.

A Life Full of Struggle

Born on March 30, 1853 in the Netherlands to a pastor's family. He was an introverted, difficult child. He worked as an art dealer, teacher, and preacher. Only at 27 did he commit fully to painting, sustained throughout by his brother Theo's unwavering support.

Paris and Arles

In Paris (1886–1888) he encountered the Impressionists and Post-Impressionists, and his palette became intensely vibrant. In 1888 he moved to Arles, where he created his most celebrated works. He invited Paul Gauguin to join him, but their relationship ended in a violent quarrel and the infamous incident of Van Gogh cutting off his own ear.

Final Years and Legacy

Van Gogh sold only one painting during his lifetime. Today his works are considered pinnacles of world art, selling for tens of millions of dollars.

Death

On July 27, 1890, aged 37, Vincent van Gogh shot himself in the chest. He died on July 29, 1890 in Auvers-sur-Oise. His brother Theo was by his side until the very end.
